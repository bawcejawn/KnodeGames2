How it works

  • One round a day (UTC). Picks open 2 days before the day and lock 5 minutes before it. No changes.
  • Tick the producers you think will beat the pool node. Every producer you leave unticked, you say the pool node beats.
  • Higher score for the day wins. A tie, or a producer with no measure that day, scores nothing for anyone.
  • 1 point for every matchup you call right.
  • Lines and multipliers stay hidden until picks lock. You see names only.
