In short

  • Knode Games are free prediction games on Koinos, a public blockchain.
  • You pick block producers, blocks or races, and correct calls climb a weekly board.
  • Playing is free, and you can start as a guest with no wallet.
  • Staking is optional, and you can withdraw your stake any time.
  • Prizes come from the vault's block rewards, not from other players, and they can be zero.

The basics

What is a blockchain?

A blockchain is a shared record book that many computers each keep a copy of, so no single company controls it. Koinos is one blockchain, its coin is called KOIN, and anyone can read its record.

What are blocks and block producers?

A block is one page of that record book, and a new one is added about every 3 seconds. Block producers are the computers that take turns making blocks, picked in proportion to their VHP.

What is a node?

A node is a computer that keeps a full copy of the record book and checks every new block. With VHP behind it, a node can also produce blocks.

What is VHP?

VHP is a Koinos token you get by burning KOIN, one for one. It lets a node produce blocks, and it is what you stake in Knode Games.

What is Mana?

Every Koinos action runs on Mana instead of a fee, and Mana comes from the KOIN in a wallet and recharges by itself. Knode Games can pay the Mana for your first pick, and changing a pick uses your own.

Playing

How do I start for free?

Open a game, tap your pick and send it as a guest, with no wallet and nothing to install. Your picks count on that game's weekly board.

Pick a game
How does a week work?

Each game keeps a weekly board: correct calls score points, and the best scores sit on top. Rewards depend on how many blocks our node produces, so a week can pay nothing.

Where do the rewards come from?

The vault collects its block rewards for the week, and a flat 5% fee is taken first. What is left pays the free-game prizes first, and the rest is shared among the staked games by each game's share of the stake.

Staking

What is staking?

Staking means putting VHP into one shared vault, and our node uses the vault's VHP to produce blocks. Your stake is held only by the vault contract, and you can always withdraw it.

How do I stake?

Get some VHP, then connect a Koinos wallet: Kondor on a computer or KOIN Vault on a phone. On the Stake page, pick a game and stake at least the minimum shown there; a new stake counts from the next week.

Can I take my stake back during a week?

Withdrawing part of your stake during a week keeps you in that week at the lower stake, as long as at least the minimum stake stays in. Withdraw below it, or all of it, and you get no share of that game's rewards that week.

Your own node

Why run your own node?

More independent nodes make the network harder to stop or cheat. Your own node also lets you read the chain yourself instead of trusting someone else's server.

What does a node need?

You need a computer that stays on, a steady internet connection, plenty of free disk space and some patience. The Run a node page walks you through it step by step.
