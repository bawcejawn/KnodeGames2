Over/Under

Over or under? Call any producer.

Every day each producer on the roster has a line at half a block, set by the VHP Ruler. Tap Over or Under on any rows you like, then send the whole day in one approval. A row you leave blank is no pick. One point for each row you call right.

How it works

The roster and the lines. The roster for each week comes out on Friday 00:00 UTC, a day before the first picks open, and never changes. It lists the producers that have the ruler's entry (not in ramp-up) and are inside the ruler's VHP range, at most 64. Each has a line: the Ruler's expected blocks for it, rounded to a half block (28.5, 29.5, …). Nobody types a line. The page checks the roster against its own checksum before it shows a row.

Your pick. Over means you think the producer will be credited with more blocks than its line that UTC day; Under means fewer. Your rows for a day are two numbers on the Koinos chain (rows 1 to 32, rows 33 to 64), sent in one approval. Blank rows are no pick. Picks open 2 days ahead. Rows 1 to 32 lock 5 minutes before the day starts; rows 33 to 64 lock 12 hours earlier.

Changes. After your first send you can change a day's picks up to 2 more times before the lock. Every change is paid with your own Mana, not the free sponsor. A played day can't be re-picked, and no pick is ever given back.

Points. 1 point for each row you called right. A producer that is void that day (its VHP left the ruler's range) is skipped: no win, no loss. A count exactly on the line is skipped too. Sunday is a fun round: it counts on Today and All time only.

Prizes. The Free play board pays 2 VHP for first and 1 VHP for second each week. Wallets with at least 1 VHP staked in Over/Under that week play on the Staked board when staking is on. Weekly boards; All time is bragging rights.

Fair checks. Days are scored from irreversible blocks that match on two independent nodes, with the VHP Ruler's own code, and every card is rebuilt and checked before results are published. The page and the recorder run the same rules file (games/overunder/math.js). Picks are public on chain before the lock.
